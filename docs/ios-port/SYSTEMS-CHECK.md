# Systems check (M5)

Every gameplay system of Anomaly driven on the Mac under AddressSanitizer + UBSan, with what the game reports back asserted from Lua, before
anyone plays the iPad by hand. This page is the table of systems, how to rerun it, what the harness can and cannot do, and what the check
found.

```bash
tools/sweep/check.sh                  # every plan on the ASan build (build/macos-arm64-asan), about 75 minutes; the report is build/check-runs/<time>/results.md
tools/sweep/check.sh --soak 60        # plus the 60-minute soak (it is only worth running on a plain build: --plain --app <release anomaly.app>)
tools/sweep/check.sh chk_weapons chk_alife        # some plans
tools/sweep/check.sh --list
```

The plans are `gamedata/scripts/sweep_check*.script` (they use the crash sweep's driver, [SANITIZERS.md](SANITIZERS.md#the-driver)); `check.sh` runs them
one after the other through `run.sh`. The first plan, `newgame`, starts a new game from the menu and saves `sweep_base`, which the others load.
Every run is unattended (`--script`: offscreen, no window, null audio, no cursor capture, never frontmost), has its own `ANOMALY_STATE_ROOT`
and is the only game of the session at that time. `check_report.py` reads each run's engine log (the `[sweep] ... CHECK PASS|FAIL system/item | detail`
lines, Lua errors), `summary.txt` (sanitizer counts), crash files, the app's footprint samples and the sound counters, and writes `results.md`;
the exit status is 1 when anything failed, the sanitizers reported, a crash file appeared or the log holds a Lua error.

Result of the last full run (on main merged into this branch, ASan + UBSan build): **296 of 296 checks of the 30 plans pass**, 0 ASan reports, 0 crash files and 0 Lua error lines in the 30 runs; the pass takes about 70 minutes. UBSan: 0 to 3 reports per run, in two plans (see "Seen and not fixed"). One step flaked in that pass (`chk_weapons`: the silenced rifle did not fire: a jam left from the step before) and was changed; the plan was rerun green (three times in a row) and the other 29 are the pass's own results. The 60-minute soak (release build, run earlier, see below): 86 cycles, no Lua error; its memory slope fails the plan's limit of 5 MB per minute (+23, an open item).

## Memory and the 60-minute soak

`chk_soak` on the release build (`tools/sweep/check.sh --soak 60 --plain --app <release anomaly.app> chk_soak`, 61 minutes of game): cycles of a walk, a fight
with a stalker, a dog and a boar, the clock at x300, a forced sleep every third cycle, the inventory and the PDA every other cycle, items made and used, and
a level change every 12 cycles (Cordon, Garbage, Agroprom, Cordon, ...).

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Cycles | 86 in 60.1 minutes, **0 raised a Lua error**; one `! ERROR get_object_by_id` print in the whole hour |
| Level changes | 7 (every load came back with a living actor, 633 to 714 squads on the board) |
| Lua heap | 43 to 57 MB all hour, no trend |
| Footprint (`phys_footprint`, every 10 s) | 3,380 MB at the start, 4,229 at 1 min, 5,137 at 18 min, 5,109 at 30, 5,133 at 51, 5,579 at 60 min; peak 5,716 MB (the staircase below) |
| Metal textures (`[ D3D ]: textures`) | 1.8 GB after the first load, 2.2 GB at 2 min, 2.56 GB by the third level, then 2.56 to 2.68 GB (about +0.1 GB at some level visits) |

Where the footprint goes, and the cause of INTEGRATION.md's "about 30 MB per minute":

- **The main loop had no autorelease pool** (fix 1 below). An idle Cordon, 12 minutes, release build: +32 MB per minute before, with the Lua heap and Metal flat;
  `malloc_history` named the Metal command encoders and `NSString`s of the render path. After the fix the live malloc total grows about 2 MB per minute (OpenAL sample buffers).
- **The sound cache is a lazily touched allocation.** `CSoundRender_Cache` callocs `snd_cache_size` MB (512 by default, `snd_cache_size` in the console) and the
  pages are charged as sounds are decoded into them, so the footprint climbs by up to that much during the first half hour of play whatever the game does. An idle Cordon with
  `snd_cache_size 16` is flat over 12 minutes; with the default it still climbs +17 MB per minute (fixed build). On the iPad (7.99 GB budget) 512 MB of it is a
  setting worth choosing deliberately.
- **Textures** are the rest: the engine keeps what it has streamed in, up to about 2.6 GB of Metal textures here. The footprint is a staircase, not a line: 3.4 GB at
  start, 4.5 GB at 15 min, then 5.0 to 5.2 GB from 18 to 33 min, 5.2 to 5.4 GB to 51 min and 5.5 to 5.6 GB at the end (a few hundred MB per three level visits as the textures
  and buffers of each level are kept; the Lua heap and the squad count do not move). The least-squares slope of the hour is +23 MB per minute, and
  most of it is the first 18 minutes. A level change itself drops about 0.6 GB of textures (1.6 GB right after `Destroying level`) and fills them again (2.3 to 2.7 GB once loaded).
  On the iPad's 7.99 GB budget the +2.2 GB of a first hour is the number to keep an eye on; this is an open item for whoever tunes `texture_lod` and the caches, not something fixed here.

The soak ran on the release build just before the ODE and `item_in_slot` fixes and the `WithAutoreleasePool` template tweak; the pool itself was in.

Seen and not fixed (none is specific to arm64):

- `alife():kill_entity(npc)` asserts (`l_tpALifeMonsterAbstract->children.empty()`) for a stalker that carries items, so the bounty task cannot be finished by killing an offline target from a script. The check kills an online stand-in instead.
- Script windows (`CUIScriptWnd`: the repair dialog, `ui_options.UIOptions`, the load dialog) keep the `lua_State` they were created on for their callbacks. Created from the driver's coroutine that is a suspended thread, and the driver's next resume fails with "cannot resume dead coroutine". `C.on_main` runs such calls from a time event on the main state. The same holds for a game script that opens one of these windows from its own coroutine (none of Anomaly's does).
- `axr_options.ltx` lives in `game/Anomaly/gamedata/configs`, because `$game_config$` is `$fs_root$\gamedata\configs`: **every run of every session shares and rewrites one file in the game folder**, including the `[character_creation]` values a new game reads. It holds `new_game_faction` and the like for the moment between "Start" and the first update only, and the runs do not overlap in practice, but it is the one piece of state `ANOMALY_STATE_ROOT` does not isolate.
- Anomalies of the dynamic-anomaly plugin (`bind_anomaly_field`) are switched off for the whole stay of the player on a level, a random share of them: four electric zones in a row did nothing. By design; `enable_anomaly()` turns one on.
- Hits are ignored for about seven seconds after an outfit is put on (health and condition unchanged for five `db.actor:hit` calls, then the sixth lands). Probably the dress animation; not looked into.
- **A level change nobody asked for, now and then (open, not reproducible on demand).** In 4 of the first 11 runs of the level chain the actor, just arrived on a level (the Agroprom after the Yantar in three runs, the Bar after the guide in one), went through a second `M_CHANGE_LEVEL` before the plan's first step: autosave, `Disconnect`, and the game loaded the Cordon at the start position of the base save (game vertex 454). The next six runs of the chain on the release build, `chk_jumps` (the same jumps without the guide, on ASan and release) and every other chain were clean, and the sender was not found: the engine log now names the destination of every level change (`* Changing level: game vertex N (level), level vertex N, from level`, `* Level changer X (silent) touched by the actor`) and the sweep logs the stack of every script call of `change_level_now` (`sweep_check.install`), so the next occurrence shows a level change no plan asked for with either the stack of the script that sent it or the changer that was touched. The suspect is the shared `axr_options.ltx` above (a new-game start request left in the file by another game running on the same game folder); that is not shown. The chain asks for its level again when it finds itself elsewhere (twice at most in a run), and the check's detail says "after N stray level change(s)" when it had to.
- `UBSan` reports on the merged tree, 0 to 3 per run (before the merge 9 to 13 per run, the renderer's, which main has fixed since): `chk_anomalies` and `chk_travel` meet a luabind call of a null `CActionBase` (a script's action planner asks for an action that is not there: `back_reference.hpp`, `call.hpp`, `action_planner_inline.h`, `operator_abstract_inline.h`), an `iFloor(NaN)` (`_bitwise.h`, the same class as [SANITIZERS.md](SANITIZERS.md#open-items) item 4) and a float division by zero in `chimera_attack_state_inline.h:268` (in a chimera's attack state). None is fixed here.

The steps of the harness that look like workarounds are what the game does: see the next section.

## How the harness works, and what it taught

Things the plans had to learn about the game, so the next plan does not have to:

- **Script slot numbers are the ini slot plus one.** `wpn_knife` has `slot = 0` in its section and is `item_in_slot(1)`; the pistol is 2, the rifle 3, grenades 4, binoculars 5, bolts 6, the outfit 7, PDA 8, detector 9, torch 10, backpack 13. `item_in_slot(n)` was unchecked for `n` above the last slot (fixed).
- **Time.** `surge_manager` puts `level.set_time_factor` back to its normal 6 every second while no emission runs, so a plan that wants the clock fast sets it every frame (`fast_forward` in the plans). The sweep's older `sleep` and `surge` plans set it once and so ran at normal speed. A surge or psi storm lasts 222 and 254 real seconds whatever the factor (the managers divide by it).
- **Keys.** `level.press_action(dik)` / `hold_action` / `release_action` go through `IR_OnKeyboardPress` / `Release`, so engine actions (fire, reload, zoom, inventory) and the Lua `on_key_release` callbacks (headlamp, night vision) both see them. Zoom and the grenade throw are hold-and-release.
- **Equipment.** The headlamp is `device_torch_dummy` (the plain `device_torch` has no power-consumption entry and the device script ignores it), night vision is `device_torch_nv_*` in the torch slot, the detector comes up with the show-detector key (`DIK_O`). `torch_enabled()` is a method of the torch object, not of the actor.
- **Weapons.** A rifle's silencer is `wpn_sil_tgp-a`, its scopes are `1p29`, `kobra`, `ps01` (`attach_scope` clones the weapon into `wpn_ak74_1p29`). `hide_weapon()` has to be undone with `restore_weapon()` before anything else can be put in the hands. A grenade thrown at 1.5 s of pin burns for 1.5 s more, so the actor is in god mode for it.
- **Mechanics.** A cleaning kit repairs weapons whose condition is between 0.75 and 0.99; an upgrade is installed by its element id (`up_firsta_ak74`, not the group), with `inventory_upgrades.force_upgrade` set because the precondition script wants a mechanic to be talking.
- **Tasks.** The dialog picks the bounty target in `axr_task_manager.trigger_job_func(task_id)` and then calls `task_manager:give_task(task_id, giver_id)`; the hand-in is `axr_task_manager.set_finished_task_complete(npc)`.
- **Companions** come from squads: `become_actor_companion` does nothing for a lone NPC made by `alife_create("sim_default_stalker_0")` (that is an NPC, not a squad); the check makes `survival_squad_stalker`, three stalkers, as `game_setup` does.
- **Cover** for emissions and sleep is a restrictor zone listed in `misc\surge_manager.ltx` (`db.zone_by_name`); the Rookie Village start is inside one. `level.vertex_in_direction` from the actor stops at the first wall, so far points come from the game graph's vertices of the level (`C.level_points`).
- **New game as another faction** is the faction dialog's `[character_creation]` values (faction, start map, story mode, money, loadout) in `axr_options.ltx`, which `run.sh` seeds from the main menu with `run_string sweep_check_play.seed(...)` and then `start server(all/single/alife/new) client(localhost)`; the game's own `on_game_load` and `actor_on_first_update` do the rest, including the level change out of `fake_start`.
- **The PDA** is a device in slot 8 and `g_3d_pda` is on by default: the `active_jobs` key (P) raises it in the hands and its screen is the UI, drawn into the device's render target (`chk_pda3d`'s screenshots). The plain dialog (`ShowDialog`, `chk_pda`) is the other mode and what a controller or the `g_3d_pda off` setting gets. `custom20`, the map key the in-hand handler of `actor_effects` listens to, has no default binding.
- **The main menu** stops the level and with it the driver, so `chk_menu` opens and closes it from the `--script` timeline (`waitlevel`, `main_menu on`, `run_string sweep_check_ui.menu_verify(...)`).
- **A Lua error is fatal here** (`SCRIPT RUNTIME ERROR`, the game stops), so a plan that logs `[sweep] FAIL phase` or a report with a Lua error line is a real finding, not noise. `check_report.py` lists them.
